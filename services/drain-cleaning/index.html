PLUMBING INSIGHTS / GENERAL INFORMATION

Understanding a blocked drain

A slow sink or recurring backup is worth describing carefully. Drain cleaning is one of McBride & Sons Plumbing’s listed services; the information below explains common plumbing concerns, not a diagnosis or a promise about any particular method.

01

Look beyond one slow fixture

Hair, soap residue, grease and other debris can restrict a drain. A recurring blockage or several slow fixtures can suggest a problem beyond a single sink or tub.

02

When a blockage needs attention

Standing water, repeated backups or wastewater coming up through another fixture are reasons to contact a plumbing professional. Avoid repeatedly adding chemical cleaners, and tell the plumber if any products have already been used.

BEFORE YOU CALL

Helpful details to share

Which fixtures are affected? When did the trouble start? Is the water draining slowly, not draining at all, or backing up elsewhere?

If wastewater is overflowing, avoid using affected fixtures while arranging professional assistance.
