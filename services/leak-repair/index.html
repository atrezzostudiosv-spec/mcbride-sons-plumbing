YOUR SERVICE, EXPLAINED

Understanding the signs of a leak

Some leaks are visible at a fixture; others first appear as moisture, staining or unexplained water use. McBride & Sons Plumbing lists leak repair as a core service. This guide helps organize what to report before a professional assessment.

GENERAL PLUMBING INFORMATION01 — 03

Leaks can waste water and create concerns for your home. Our published services include diagnosing and repairing hidden pipe leaks and visible fixture issues.

02 / PLUMBING INSIGHTS

Small signs can be useful clues

Damp cabinets, persistent dripping, water marks or an unexpected rise in water usage can indicate a leak. The location of visible moisture does not always identify its source.

Discuss your service needs
03 / PLUMBING INSIGHTS

Responding to active water loss

If water is flowing uncontrollably, the priority is reducing exposure to the water where it is safe to do so. Know the location of the water shutoff, keep clear of electrical hazards, and seek professional assistance.
