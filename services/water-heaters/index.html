PLUMBING INSIGHTS / GENERAL INFORMATION

When to ask about your water heater

Water heaters are an essential part of everyday plumbing. McBride & Sons Plumbing lists water heater services, but the source does not specify individual heater types or a complete menu of procedures. Confirm the available work directly when contacting the company.

01

Changes worth noticing

Water temperature that fluctuates, unusually limited hot water, unfamiliar noises or visible moisture around a water heater are useful details to report.

02

Safety comes before troubleshooting

Leaking water, overheating concerns or an unusual odor warrant caution. Do not open equipment or attempt electrical or gas repairs yourself; follow manufacturer safety instructions and contact an appropriate professional.

BEFORE YOU CALL

Helpful details to share

Is the concern temperature, water quantity, noise or leakage? What type of unit is present, if known? When did the change begin?
