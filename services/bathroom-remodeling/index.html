PLUMBING INSIGHTS / GENERAL INFORMATION

Planning the plumbing side of a bathroom update

Bathroom remodeling can involve fixtures, connections and plumbing-layout decisions. McBride & Sons Plumbing lists plumbing support for bathroom remodeling; this is not a claim that the company provides complete design, cabinetry, flooring or general contracting.

01

Start with fixture locations

Knowing which plumbing fixtures may remain in place and which may move helps frame the conversation. A change in layout can affect supply and drainage requirements.

02

Coordinate plumbing before finishes

Planning the plumbing portion early can help identify questions about access, existing connections and fixture compatibility before finished surfaces are installed. Exact project scope needs confirmation.

BEFORE YOU CALL

Helpful details to share

Which plumbing fixtures are changing? Are their locations staying the same? Is the bathroom already under renovation?

Discuss the plumbing scope separately from any non-plumbing remodeling work.
